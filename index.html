How to Use Lifts
👋 This app remembers your workouts so you can see if you're getting stronger. Here's everything, nice and simple.
Doing a Workout
1️⃣
Pick what you'll do
When you open the app, you see a list of exercises. Tap the ones you want to do today. They light up. Then tap the big START WORKOUT button.
2️⃣
Write down what you lifted
For each set, type how heavy (weight) and how many times you lifted it (reps). That's it — two little boxes.
3️⃣
Tap the circle when a set is done
See the little ○ next to each set? Tap it after you finish that set. It turns into a green ✓ and a rest timer pops up to count down your break.
4️⃣
Tap LOG WORKOUT when you're finished
This saves everything. You'll see a fun summary of what you did. If you lifted more than ever before, you get confetti! 🎉
Follow a Program (Optional)
📚
Let the app plan for you
Tap 📚 at the top and pick a program like StrongLifts 5×5, GZCLP, or 5/3/1. Type your starting weight (or training max for 5/3/1) for each lift once. From then on, the start screen shows your next workout day — tap it and every set is pre-filled with the exact weight and reps to do.
📈
The weight goes up by itself
Finish all your sets and log the workout — the app adds weight for next time automatically. Miss your reps a few sessions in a row and it backs the weight off (a "deload") so you can build back up. On 5/3/1, your training max goes up at the end of each 4-week cycle instead. You'll see the new weights on the finish screen.
🔥
Warm-up sets
On any exercise, tap 🔥 Warm-ups and the app adds a few lighter build-up sets leading to your working weight. They don't count toward your totals or records.
While You're Working Out
🎯
Focus mode (one exercise at a time)
Workouts open with a single exercise filling the screen so you can concentrate on the lift in front of you. Use Next › or the strip of upcoming exercises along the bottom to move through the session — it also jumps ahead on its own when you finish one. Prefer to see everything at once? Tap ☰ All for the full list (and ⊡ Focus mode to switch back).
⏱️
The rest timer
When you tap a set's circle to finish it, a big countdown pops up so you know when to start your next set. Tap +30s for more time, or Skip to start early. It beeps when time's up.
✎
Forgot an exercise?
Tap ✎ Edit exercises in the green bar at the top to go back and add or remove exercises. Your numbers stay saved.
🛑
The End button
Changed your mind and don't want to save? Tap End to throw away today's entries and go back to the start.
🔥
Your streak
At the top you'll see how many days in a row you've worked out, and how many times this week. Keep it going!
The Buttons on Each Exercise
➕
+ Set and − Set
Did more sets than the boxes shown? Tap + Set for another row. Did fewer? Tap − Set to take one away.
✓
Complete
Done with the whole exercise? Tap ✓ Complete and it checks off every set at once and tidies the card away.
↺
↺ (the loopy arrow)
Fills in the same weights and reps you did last time, so you don't have to remember. Then just change anything that's different.
📝
📝 (the notepad)
Tap it to leave yourself a little note, like "felt easy" or "sore shoulder."
Exercises With No Weight
💪
Things like push-ups or planks
You don't lift any weight — it's just your body. So leave the weight box empty and only type the reps. The app understands.
⚙️
Tell the app how much you weigh
Tap the gear (⚙️) at the top and type your body weight. Then the app can still figure out how hard those exercises were.
Helpful Extras
📋
Templates (saved workouts)
Do the same workout a lot? On the start screen, tap the exercises you want, then tap "Save / manage templates," type a name like "Leg Day," and hit Save. Next time, your saved workout shows up as a button at the top of the start screen — tap it and everything's ready.
🔢
Plate helper
Not sure which weights to put on the bar? Tap 🔢, type the total weight you want, and it shows you exactly which plates to grab.
📅
History tab
See every workout you've ever done. Tap one to open it up.
📈
Progress tab
Pick an exercise to see a line going (hopefully!) up over time. That means you're getting stronger.
↔
Move an exercise
In the Exercises tab, tap the ↔ button next to any exercise to move it to a different group (like Upper Body to Lower Body). All its history comes with it.
✏️
Rename an exercise
Tap the ✏️ button to give an exercise a new name. Its past workouts keep counting under the new name.
⭐ Don't Lose Your Stuff!
💾
Save a copy (Backup)
Go to the Exercises tab and tap Backup to Drive. This makes a little file. Put that file somewhere safe like Google Drive. Do this every week or so.
📲
Get your stuff back (Restore)
Got a new phone, or something went wrong? Tap Restore from Drive and pick that saved file. Everything comes back.
💡 Easy habit: finish your workout → tap Log Workout → tap Backup. Done. Your hard work is safe.
Put It on Your Phone Like an App
📱
Add to Home Screen
Open this in Chrome, tap the three dots (⋮) at the top, and pick "Add to Home Screen." Now it sits on your phone with all your other apps and opens with one tap.
